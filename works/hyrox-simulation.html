Project Overview

Diivoke was on-site in Gainesville, FL to capture live coverage of an actual Hyrox race, following athletes from station to station as they competed.

The goal was to document the intensity of race day in a way that felt authentic to the sport and the energy of the room.

On Location

We moved between stations throughout the race — sled pushes, sprints, and finish-line moments — capturing athletes pushing through fatigue in real time.

Footage was shot handheld to stay close to the action and keep the pace of the event front and center.

Final Outcome

The finished footage captures the raw energy of race day, giving fresh, high-energy content to promote future Hyrox races in Gainesville, FL.
